Speech is the human act of communicating thoughts, feelings, or information through spoken language, utilizing vocal folds, breath, and articulation to produce sound. It is a fundamental method of verbal communication, often delivered as a formal address or public presentation to engage an audience through storytelling, data, or rhetorical questions.

Key aspects of speech include:
Definition: Speech is the ability to talk, a specific manner of speaking, or a formal discourse. It involves using the larynx, tongue, lips, and teeth to produce sounds.
Types of Speeches: These include informative (educating on a topic), persuasive (convincing an audience), and motivational speeches, such as personal narratives about overcoming challenges.
Effective Delivery: Starting a speech effectively can involve storytelling, using a striking fact, or asking a rhetorical question to immediately capture attention.
Practice: Effective methods for practicing a speech include the "3x3+1" method (3 repetitions per day for 3 days, plus one on the day of).
Speech Therapy: In healthcare, speech therapy assists with disorders or rehabilitation, often focusing on improving communication abilities
